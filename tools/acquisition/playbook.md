# Negotiation Playbook — ARIA Acquisition Agent

REQUIRED knowledge file for the draft stage (`reply/draft.py` loads this
verbatim into the drafting prompt). Encoded from live deals. The reply agent
follows these rules when drafting; it NEVER sends — every outbound offer goes
through the human review queue.

## Rule 0 — Verification before any written number. Always.

No dollar figure enters a draft until the verification routine has run against
county records for THIS parcel: ownership matches the person replying, legal
access checked, flood checked, comps pulled, offer box computed. If
verification is incomplete or stale, the draft is a HOLDING message (see
templates) with a self-imposed deadline — and no number, not a range, not a
ballpark, not "around".

## Rule 1 — Seller archetypes and handling

**Fresh-basis investor** (tenure < 2 years — they bought recently and know
their number): One honest number, first message. No dance. Expect a pass;
close with the backup-buyer line: "If your plans change or another deal falls
through, my offer stands — I close in ~30 days whenever you're ready."

**Aged individual holder** (tenure 5+ years, individual/couple on deed):
Direct number plus the net-cash bridge — walk from the county assessed value
or any listing estimate they cite to NET cash in pocket: minus commission,
closing costs, the year of carrying taxes while listed. Our number is smaller
but it is net, now, and certain.

**Heirs / estate**: The pitch is a frictionless close, not the price: e-sign,
fully remote, mobile notary, title company splits proceeds per the estate.
Always ask early: "Is everyone on the deed (or in the estate) on board with
selling?" — uncovers the co-signer problem before a contract, not after.

**Informed multi-lot holder**: Qualify before pricing: what competing offers
do they hold, how many parcels, which ones. Package pricing when it helps.
If our diligence found defects (flood, access, slivers), FULL disclosure of
the teardown — informed sellers respect the work and it anchors the price
honestly.

**Terms-savvy seller** (asks about terms, financing, creative structure):
Acknowledge terms interest without agreeing to anything in writing;
dispo-first before contracting — check buyer depth for the structure before
we sign anything.

## Rule 2 — Never cite the seller's own purchase history or deed chain

We may KNOW their basis from records; we never say it. Quoting a seller's own
purchase price or deed history to them reads as surveillance and kills trust.
Bridges are built from assessed value, listing math, or comps — never their
chain of title.

## Rule 3 — Assessed-value bridge (TX growth counties)

For TX counties with fast-rising CAD assessments: "The county's assessed
value is a tax number, not a market number — assessments lag or lead sales
depending on the cycle. Here's what parcels like yours actually traded for:
[comps]. My offer nets you $X with zero costs."

## Rule 4 — Honest-pass letter

When diligence kills the deal, say so plainly: name the defect (landlocked,
sliver, floodway), point to better exits — the adjacent owners (name them if
we pulled them), the county, a local agent. Include a standing floor number
ONLY if it is genuinely performable via assignment at that price. An honest
pass today is a referral or a callback next year.

## Rule 5 — Decision-rule footer (every draft, for the approver)

Every draft ends with a footer FOR JUSTIN (stripped before send):

    ── approval guide ─────────────────────────
    SIGN below:      $<mao>        (max allowable offer)
    COUNTER once at: $<open_at>    (opening number in draft)
    WALK above:      $<walk_at>    (ceiling minus minimum fee)
    evidence: <comps cited> · <flags> · verified <date>

## Rule 6 — Holding messages

When we owe the seller a number but verification isn't done: send a holding
message with a SELF-IMPOSED deadline ("I'll have a firm number to you by
Thursday"). The system tracks that deadline and surfaces it in the review
queue — deadlines we set are deadlines we honor.

## Rule 7 — Compliance (every draft, non-negotiable)

- Compliance footer with physical address and a working opt-out on every email.
- STOP/unsubscribe: processed automatically and instantly, no approval needed.
- No marketing of the property itself (TX Occ. Code 1101.0045) — we express
  interest in contracting to buy, with disclosure; templates never advertise
  a property publicly.
- DNC-flagged phones are never called or texted.
